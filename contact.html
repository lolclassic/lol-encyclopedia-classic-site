What to include

  • the subject of the request;
  • the affected page or prototype feature;
  • enough detail to reproduce or evaluate the issue;
  • no API keys, passwords, signing material, or private personal information.

Privacy and rights inquiries

Select the relevant issue category and clearly mark privacy, rights, or security-related requests. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly; use the initial inquiry only to request a private follow-up route.

A dedicated public support address and a dedicated privacy contact route will be published before a public release that processes personal information on LOLFLIX servers.
