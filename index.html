LoL Encyclopedia Classic — Unofficial Archive is an independently developed Android reference app for exploring historical champions, items, masteries, summoner spells, and runes, with a pseudonymous online community.

Implemented today

A historical archive and community in one app.

The app includes searchable champion, item, rune, and terminology references. Posts, comments, recommendations, reporting, blocking, and bug reports are handled by an HTTPS online service.

01

60 historical champions

Browse fixed historical portraits, names, details, and normalized P/Q/W/E/R skill information.

02

Items and build paths

Explore 190 historical items with categories, prices, component items, and connected build paths.

03

Masteries and spells

Review 56 masteries and 16 classic summoner spells.

04

Classic rune client

Save 20 customizable rune pages with a 30-slot page layout on the device.

05

Pseudonymous community

Users aged 16 or older can create a pseudonymous profile, post, comment, recommend, report, and block or unblock users.

Data boundary

Local reference data stays separate from the online community.

App settings, masteries, builds, and rune pages remain on the device. Community data is sent only to the LOLFLIX online service over HTTPS.

On device

Local archive

  • Historical references and preferences
  • Mastery choices, builds, and rune pages
  • Removable through app data deletion
  • No ads, analytics SDK, purchases, or subscriptions
HTTPS online processing

Community and moderation

  • Pseudonymous profiles and nicknames
  • Posts, comments, recommendations, reports, blocks, and bug reports
  • Age 16+ confirmation and acceptance of the 2026-08-02 terms
  • Moderator review and content management

Project identity

Independent, unofficial historical reference app.

LoL Encyclopedia Classic — Unofficial Archive is free, independently developed, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games. “Classic” describes the historical archive theme; it does not claim to be an official League of Legends Classic product.
